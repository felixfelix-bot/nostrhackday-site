Sign up with proof of work

No accounts, no server. Your RSVP is a nostr event (kind 1337) signed by a key generated in this tab that never leaves it. To be counted it must carry real work: a leet npub prefix you can check with your own eyes (n05…) plus a NIP-13 nonce meeting the current rung.

The counter is live — an applesauce EventStore fed by public relays. Verification also runs locally: the id is recomputed, the signature checked, the leading zero bits counted. The declared nonce is never trusted.

    MINING YOUR NPUB live proof of work

    Right now your browser is grinding real nostr keypairs on every core it can grab, and keeping the one whose npub spells the leet target. Every matched character is 5 bits of real work: the mined prefix IS the proof. The grid below shows the candidate being tried right now — one fixed colour per character. The word's second half cannot be spelled at all, so instead your npub must contain a raindrop: any 9-character window with at most 6 distinct characters.
