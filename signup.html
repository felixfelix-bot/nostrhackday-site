Sign up with proof of work

No accounts, no emails, no server. Your RSVP is a nostr event (kind 1337) signed by a key that is generated here in your browser and never leaves it. To be counted it must carry real, verifiable work: a leet npub prefix you can check with your own eyes (n05…) plus a NIP-13 nonce that meets the current ladder rung.

The counter below is live: it is an applesauce EventStore fed by public relays and read through a reactive query, so accepted RSVPs and the current required difficulty update as events land. Verification runs in your browser too — the event id is recomputed, the signature is checked, and the leading zero bits are counted locally. The declared nonce value is never trusted.
